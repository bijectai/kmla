   positions are those "bound at every call site in the statutes and case
   queries" and resolves `nonvar`/`var` guards by mode. It does not say whether
   a call site counts when every path to it, in that mode, first passes a goal
   that raises an instantiation error or has no success path.
   - Reading S counts such a site; reading L does not.
   - They differ on 10 rows: `s152_b_2/4`, `s152_c_1_E/3`, `s152_c_3/3`,
     `s152_d_1_D/2`, `s3306_c_10_A_ii/3`, `s7703_a_1/5`, `s7703_a_2/5`,

     `section3306.pl:317` (F9/E2, `DECISIONS.md:1524`) or the unbound-Workday
     path (F4). G6 excludes those inputs from `Valid`.

   refinements of L change further rows:

   reference-undefined (always-raising) evaluations are also excluded, these

3. **Other G2 interpretations** the derivation assumed; please confirm or
   correct each:
   - **Earlier statute calls:** a variable bound by an earlier statute call
     counts as "bound at the call site" if every clause binds it and no
     stipulation leaves it unbound. Four `s2` inputs depend on this
     (`s2_a_1_B/4` 1, `s2_a_2_A/5` 1, `s2_a_2_B/3` 1, `s2_b_1_A_i_II/3` 2), as
     does position 1 of the 20 `s1_*_{i..v}/2` rows.
   - **Beyond the corpus:** those classifications rely on corpus stipulations
     being ground in the position, for example all 60 `s63/3` stipulations in
     position 3 (`DECISIONS.md:708`). Must they hold for Households outside the
     corpus with wildcard stipulations?
   - **Calls inside `findall/3` and `\+`:** these count as call sites.
     `s151_b/4` 2 and `s151_c/4` 2 depend on the findall case, and
     `s3306_a_3/4` 4 on the `\+` case.
   - **Guard-emptied calls:** a call whose callee is the both-unbound `s152`
     empty list still counts as a call site. `s152_b_1/3` position 1 depends on
     this, since R4's sketch binds it.
   - **`==` with one side unbound:** `X == Y` with X unbound is resolved false by
     mode, the complement of G3's `\==` rule. Position 1 of `s152_c_1_B`,
     `s152_c_1_E` and `s152_c_3` depends on this.
   - **Harness calls:** H4.2 grounding calls and the harness's H6.2

   - **F10 route:** `s2_a_1_A/5` position 1 is an output only through

   - **`s63_d_2/3` position 2:** the list argument in "falls under section

     omits it.
   - **Stipulated rules:** a stipulated rule whose head variable is bound by
     `between/3` in its body counts as binding that position. For example,
     `cases/s2_b_1_A_pos.pl:40`.
