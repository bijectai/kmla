# A-004

## Answer

The recommended content of `human/DECISIONS.md` is in
`docs/consult/DECISIONS_RECOMMENDED.md`: every schema section filled, every
hazard site (150 `\+`, 2 `!`, 43 `findall`, 12 `sum_list`, 101 `is`, 139
date-comparison sites) listed with `file:line:col` and an intended
translation, plus the Household field list, the stipulation inventory, the
135 queried targets with modes, `Valid`, and worked examples. Each entry has
an identifier; the whole document is PROPOSED until Dev installs it.

The decisions that matter most, in order:

1. **Time zone is a semantic input.** The reference program agrees with all
   376 SARA labels only under `TZ=America/New_York`; under UTC two cases
   fail. Under that pin the `+1 day` inside `day_to_stamp` is undone for
   every `format_time` observation, so §7703(b)(3) checks Jul 1 … Dec 31 and
   week numbers are `%W` of the actual day. (D8, F1; P-TZ.)
2. **Money is `Int` dollars**, rounding is half away from zero, and the
   float formulas in §1 and §3301 agree with exact rational evaluation on the
   whole Valid range (argued and bulk-verified on the pinned interpreter).
   The plan's "cents" is a PROPOSED change; the cents variant is spelled out
   if Dev keeps it. (M1–M7; P-MONEY.)
3. **Dates are day counts**, `is_before` is `≤`, every duration comparison
   reduces to whole days (half-year rule: `2·(end − start) ≥ Dec31 − Jan1`),
   and every predicate's interval convention is tabulated. (D1–D7.)
4. **Predicates translate to ordered solution lists**; `findall` counts
   solutions with multiplicity (three matching `location_` facts triple an
   employee's wages, verified); the external observation of a target is the
   solution *set*; scalar entry points take the first solution. (G1, A1,
   H6.)
5. **Stipulations are appended clauses**, exactly as in Prolog; cases are
   grounded by enumeration in the pinned interpreter; the two unterminated
   `s3306_c_2` files are read with the terminator restored; models are
   graded on stipulation-free inputs and, on the originals, on the
   stipulation-independent subset. This resolves B005. (H4, H6; P-STIP,
   P-TARGETS.)
6. **The reference is partial.** Five reference-undefined regions were
   found and reproduced (type error in §3306(c)(5)(B); instantiation error
   in §3306(b)(2) for plans with other beneficiaries; non-termination on
   kinship cycles, on domestic services in a USA private home, and on a
   head-of-household path through a supported parent). `Valid` excludes
   them, year-indexed. (G6, V4–V8; P-VALID-YEAR.)
7. Four statute arms are unreachable from base facts because `s3306_c/5`
   never binds `Workday` (§3306(a)(1)(B), (a)(2)(B), (c)(1)(A)(ii)); this is
   a coverage finding for Week 2, not something to generate around. (F4.)

## Rationale

DECISIONS.md is the one artifact that no test can confirm, so the only
defensible method is to (a) decide nothing that can be observed instead, and
(b) observe on the pinned interpreter, not on memory of SWI-Prolog. Every
interpreter claim above was probed on the image with the pinned version
(argument parsing is broken under emulation on this host, so probes were
driven through stdin; that itself is recorded for the harness). Where the
source is buggy (F10, F11, F16, F17) the decision is to translate the bug,
because the verdicts are relative to the oracle, not to the statute.
